# ADR 002 — Catalogue dispositions and conditional model boundaries

Accepted 13 September 2026.

The normative existence contract is extended with `false_positive`. A Kepler
false-positive disposition is not a claim that a paper was formally retracted.
The exact archive category and source remain visible. The `pl_controv_flag`
qualifies a currently confirmed catalogue object as a disputed interpretation
without deleting its measurements or history.

Reference-specific PS defaults are the initial adopted parameter sets. Cumulative
Kepler fits and other PS publications remain separate alternatives. A different
parameter solution does not change the latest reviewed existence assessment.

The engine deliberately distinguishes evidence support from environmental model
availability. The current physically bounded scenario models are conditional;
they do not produce posterior probabilities. A detailed renderer does not grant
the `observation_constrained` label. The source-driven placeholder and partial
constraint paths are complete scientific outcomes for their admitted evidence,
while unavailable retrieval/chemistry models are disclosed as implementation
limits rather than claims that no observations exist.
