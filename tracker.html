SCIENTIFIC BOUNDARY

A teaching instrument, not a navigation solution.

The visualisation turns catalogue coordinates into a legible local frame and exposes selection effects, provenance, and missing values. It does not propagate full astrometric covariance, binary orbits, radial motion, light-time, relativistic frames, or spacecraft trajectory constraints. For assessed research, query the cited archives and carry their uncertainties and selection functions through the analysis.
