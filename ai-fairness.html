AI & Fairness

How Adriano To The Star uses AI and monitors fairness

Where AI Is Used

This site uses AI in focused places to enhance the experience: Stellar AI chat, smart search suggestions, and natural-language discovery.

Fairness & Governance

  • Text Classification: User text is classified to monitor sensitive patterns consistently across all features.
  • Bias Detection: Many AI events attach a fairness object summarizing model health and simple bias metrics.
  • Safety Posture: Automatic audits check that core AI governance features are available on every relevant page.

Developer Tools

Maintainers can use built-in tools to inspect AI behavior, including the AI Usage Overview and AI Fairness Overview cards in the Analytics Dashboard.
