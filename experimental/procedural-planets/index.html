Controls and scientific limits

Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to approach the surface. Geometry refines near the camera; fine material detail is evaluated per pixel.

These are fictional procedural geographies, not observed exoplanet maps. Spectroscopy does not resolve continents, mountains, oceans or cities at Google Earth detail.
