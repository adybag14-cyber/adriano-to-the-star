Model and rendering

A pressure-projected 2D fluid field drives a volumetric reconstruction with emission, scattering and dust extinction. This is a synthetic visual study, not an observed nebula or a full astrophysical magnetohydrodynamics simulation.

High and Ultra increase solver resolution, pressure iterations and ray integration samples. Changing resolution restarts the same seed.
